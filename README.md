One short line about office life — the whole entry. A judge model reads entries
two at a time against a published brief and keeps the funnier one.

## Entry

> I hit reply-all to ask them to stop replying-all, and now 400 people are replying-all to agree with me.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (103 characters, UTF-8, within the 1–200 gate).
